By Vlad Polishchuk , Senior Underwriting Manager at Parsyl News of President Donald Trump's tariffs is reverberating around financial markets and board rooms. Because tariffs affect the cost of imported goods, there are significant implications for cargo insurance, from valuation challenges to shifting trade routes. Global trading companies, insurance brokers and cargo underwriters will need to understand the five ways their insurance policies are impacted by tariffs.
